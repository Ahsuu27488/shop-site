# Brief

**Type:** portfolio piece (track 1) + real site for the owner's family shop.
Not a paid client job.

## Goal

- Let local customers send their grocery list on WhatsApp in one tap.
- Show foreign clients a real, bilingual, mobile-first business site.

## Agreed scope

- Urdu-first page with an English version, no prices (rates change daily).
- Product categories, order builder → WhatsApp, address/hours/map, delivery note.
